# Genre: Literary Fiction

## reader promise
Depth over plot machinery: psychological insight, a distinctive prose voice, and
illumination of the human condition. Readers come for character interiority, theme,
and language that rewards attention — the pleasure is recognition and resonance more
than "what happens next." The promise is that the book means something and says it
beautifully.

## conventions / obligatory scenes
- A protagonist with rich interiority and a recognisable internal contradiction.
- Theme carried through image, structure, and subtext (not stated outright).
- A precipitating change that pressures the character's sense of self.
- Scenes that turn on emotional/psychological shifts more than external events.
- An ending that resonates (often quiet, ambiguous, or epiphanic) rather than resolving.

## what successful stories do
- comps: Marilynne Robinson (*Gilead*), Colm Tóibín, Sally Rooney, Hanya Yanagihara,
  Jhumpa Lahiri. Voice and interiority are the draw — study the sentence.
- Plot is character; the external events exist to apply pressure to the interior.
- They trust the reader — subtext, white space, ambiguity over explanation.
- Prose carries weight: precise, controlled, distinctive (but not purple).
- Structure can be unconventional (fragmented, non-linear) if it serves the theme.

## tropes to honour
- the unreliable narrator, the return home, the marriage/family under strain, the
  reckoning with the past, the coming-of-age, the quiet epiphany.

## cliches to avoid
- "literary" used as licence for purple prose / no momentum;
- theme stated baldly instead of dramatised;
- interiority that's static navel-gazing (the character must still be pressured/change);
- profundity-by-vagueness (mistaking confusion for depth);
- a plotless drift with no internal escalation.

## structure note
Story Circle / character-arc structures fit best; even a "quiet" book needs internal
escalation (the pressure on the self must rise). Tension here is emotional, not plot —
the SCENE LEDGER's `moves` carry the weight; let tension track internal stakes.

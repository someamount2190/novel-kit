# Genre knowledge base — INDEX

Curated baseline of each genre's reader promise, obligatory scenes/conventions (the
genre "SOP"), what successful stories do (exemplars + shared moves), tropes to honour,
and genre-specific clichés to avoid. `genre.py` classifies a premise and pulls the
matching entry; SOP-10 augments it with LIVE research (current comps, what readers
actually praise) and records verified findings back here (anti-poison: only what a
source confirmed).

Seeded:
- [mystery](mystery.md) — fair-play puzzle; restored justice; clue ledger
- [thriller](thriller.md) — relentless escalation; competent hero vs active threat; clock
- [romance](romance.md) — emotional catharsis; GUARANTEED HEA/HFN; dual arcs
- [fantasy](fantasy.md) — coherent secondary world; magic with costs; mythic quest
- [science-fiction](science-fiction.md) — one rigorous novum followed to consequence
- [literary-fiction](literary-fiction.md) — interiority, voice, theme over plot
- [isekai](isekai.md) — portal/summoned-hero wish-fulfilment; WEAK-hero variant = earned underdog rise (researched 2026-06-21)

Not yet seeded (classifier detects → research live, then add an entry):
horror, historical. Also: western, YA, cosy, romantasy, grimdark, cyberpunk,
magical-realism — add as needed via SOP-10.

Format (H2 headings `genre.py` reads, verbatim):
`## reader promise` · `## conventions / obligatory scenes` ·
`## what successful stories do` · `## tropes to honour` · `## cliches to avoid` ·
`## structure note`

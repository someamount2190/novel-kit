# Novel Build Plan

## LOGLINE
River warden Mara Vance must find who opened the flood-gates that drowned her sister, or watch the town bury the truth with the dead.

## PREMISE & THEME
A grieving woman investigates a flood she believes was deliberate. Dramatic question: will Mara expose the truth even when it implicates the people she loves? Theme: grief curdles into obsession unless it is shared; the cost of needing to be right.

## AUDIENCE & COMPS
Literary mystery for adult readers who liked "Tana French meets The Dry." ~85k words, single POV, slow-burn.

## CHARACTERS
Mara Vance (protagonist) and Elias Roan (antagonist) — full sheets (traits, backstory, voice, want/need, arc) live in the story bible. Mara wants to name who opened the gates; needs to forgive herself. Elias wants the dam quietly decommissioned; needs forgiveness without confessing.

## STRUCTURE
Three-act. Act I ends when Mara finds the gate log altered (commits her to investigate). Midpoint: she learns her sister knew. Act II ends with the false ally (Elias) exposed. Climax: Mara chooses truth over the town's silence at the inquest.

## FLAGS
- introduction | Mara | raised @1 | paid @4 — the warden who blames herself, planted at the flood; pays when she chooses to share the truth
- death | Elias | raised @3 | broken @4 — flagged as a man with everything to lose; he is ruined, not killed (deliberate)

## SUBPLOTS
### Mara: carrying the truth alone -> sharing it
- beat: insists on investigating alone, rebuffs help @scene 2
- beat: testifies publicly, names Elias @scene 4 (resolution)

### Elias: procedure absolves me -> I chose this
- beat: hides behind regulation, lies to Mara @scene 3
- beat: breaks on the stand and confesses @scene 4 (resolution)

## SETUP/PAYOFF
- setup: the gate log is altered @ch2 => payoff: the altered log convicts Elias at the inquest @ch4
- setup: Mara never swears => payoff: she swears once, at the climax

## POV & TENSE
Third-person limited, past tense, Mara only. No head-hopping; each chapter stays in her perception.

## RESEARCH & FAILURE MODES
Researched: flood-control sluice operation, coroner's inquest procedure 1890s. Guard against: sagging middle (each Act II scene must raise tension), info-dump on dam mechanics (reveal through action), and the cliché grief montage.

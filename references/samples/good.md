# Chapter 1

The rain had stopped by the time Mara reached the bridge. Water still moved beneath it, brown and quick, carrying a child's shoe and the broken slats of someone's fence toward the sea. She stood at the rail and counted the things the river had taken. It was a habit older than grief.

A man waited at the far end. He did not look at her. He looked at the water as though it owed him an answer, and when he spoke his voice was almost lost beneath the current.

"They found the car," he said. "Two miles down. Empty."

Mara's hands tightened on the cold iron. Somewhere behind them a gull complained and wheeled away. She thought of all the things she might say, and chose none of them, and the silence between them filled with rushing water until it felt like the only honest thing left in the world.

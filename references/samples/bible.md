# Story Bible: The River Warden

## CHARACTERS
### Mara Vance
- aliases: Mara, Ms. Vance, the warden
- pronouns: she/her
- eyes: grey
- hair: black
- role: protagonist
- traits: stubborn, guilt-ridden, methodical, distrustful of authority
- backstory: Her younger sister drowned in the flood she was meant to prevent; ten years a river warden, she blames herself.
- physicality: lean and weather-burned, grey eyes, black hair scraped back hard; stands with her arms crossed; rubs an old rope-scar across her palm when she's deciding something.
- voice: clipped; nautical metaphors; never swears
- wants: name who opened the gates
- needs: forgive herself for surviving
- arc: from "the truth is mine to carry alone" to "the truth must be shared to mean anything"

### Elias Roan
- aliases: Elias, Roan
- pronouns: he/him
- eyes: brown
- role: antagonist
- traits: proud, procedural, cowardly under pressure
- backstory: The engineer who signed off on the failing dam and has hidden the fault for a decade.
- physicality: soft-handed and fastidious, brown eyes that slide off yours; straightens the objects on a desk while he talks; flinches at raised voices.
- voice: over-formal; hides behind regulation; never raises his voice
- wants: decommission the dam quietly
- needs: to be forgiven without confessing
- arc: from "procedure absolves me" to "I chose this"

## WORLD
- setting: Harkness, a coastal town
- time period: 1890s

## WORLDBUILDING
- look & feel: a fog-grey fishing town where the river meets the sea; tarred jetties, gull-noise, the constant smell of salt and silt and fish-gut.
- daily life: lives run on the tide table and the cannery whistle; everyone is owed money by everyone; the dam upstream is both the town's pride and its quiet dread.
- social order: the cannery owners and the harbour master above; fishing families below; the warden answers to the town council that the dam's engineer also sits on.
- texture details: the flood-line stains painted on the seawall; brine-stiff oilskins; coppers and shillings; the warden's brass tide-bell.

## SETUP/PAYOFF
- setup: the gate log is altered => payoff: the altered log convicts Elias at the inquest

## FORBIDDEN
- the word "okay"

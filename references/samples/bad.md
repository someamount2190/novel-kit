# Chapter 1

Sarah saw the door. She heard a noise. She felt very afraid. She knew something was wrong.

Her heart pounded as a shiver ran down her spine. Time stood still. She felt the blood run cold in her veins.

"Stop!" she exclaimed angrily. "You can't do this!" he growled. "Please," she sobbed, "don't." He laughed, "Too late."

She walked slowly to the window. She looked carefully outside. She noticed the rain. She wondered what to do. She decided to run.

***

He was really very angry. He was just so completely furious that he literally could not speak. She was extremely nervous and incredibly anxious.

The silence was deafening. Little did she know, it was the calm before the storm. She let out a breath she didn't know she was holding.

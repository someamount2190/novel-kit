## The Log

Mara climbed the records-office stairs. Her grey eyes were tired.

The clerk did not look up. "Records are closed," he said.

"Closed to whom?" she asked. The ledger lay open on the desk, and her gaze caught the entry for the night of the flood. Someone had scraped the ink away and written over it.

Her green eyes narrowed. "This is altered," she said. "Okay."

Later, Marra found Elias at the dam.
